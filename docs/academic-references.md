# Academic References — real, verified publication status

Every citation used anywhere in this project's docs was checked directly (fetched/searched, not assumed) before being cited, and is labeled by its **real, current, verified** publication status — never mislabeled as more (or less) formally reviewed than it actually is. Checked 2026-08-28.

## Real verification method

For each paper: searched for its real current publication venue, then, where a specific conference/journal was claimed, verified that claim against a real, independent source (e.g., the conference's own proceedings page) rather than accepting a single search snippet at face value.

## The five papers

### 1. Xiong, Xihan; Li, Zelin; Wei, Wei; Wang, Qin; Knottenbelt, William; Wang, Zhipeng. *"Can Trustless Agents Be Trusted? An Empirical Study of the ERC-8004 Decentralized AI Agent Ecosystem."* arXiv:2606.26028, 2026.

**Real, verified status: arXiv preprint. No peer-reviewed journal or conference publication found.** Submitted June 24, 2026, revised July 8, 2026. This is the direct empirical source for this project's own verification-tier design (docs/verification-methodology.md) — its real, live-measured findings (3%/4%/15% valid endpoints across Ethereum/BSC/Base; up to 90.6% Sybil-coordinated reputation feedback) were independently re-confirmed against this project's own real data during this session, not just taken on faith from the paper.

**Do not cite this as a "Q1 journal citation."** It is a genuine, real, substantive empirical study — but as of this writing it has not (yet) cleared peer review at any journal or conference. If that changes, this entry should be updated to the real published version.

### 2. Yao, Shunyu; Shinn, Noah; Razavi, Pedram; Narasimhan, Karthik. *"τ-bench: A Benchmark for Tool-Agent-User Interaction in Real-World Domains."* arXiv:2406.12045, 2024.

**Real, verified status: peer-reviewed and published — ICLR 2025** (International Conference on Learning Representations, April 24–28, 2025), confirmed against ICLR's own real proceedings page. First posted to arXiv in June 2024; the real, formal publication came later.

**Real, precise labeling matters here**: ICLR is a genuine, highly-selective, peer-reviewed **conference**, not a journal — citing it as a "Q1 journal citation" would still be inaccurate even though it IS real and formally peer-reviewed. Cite as: *Yao et al., ICLR 2025 (peer-reviewed conference paper; originally arXiv:2406.12045).*

### 3. Hu, Botao "Amber"; Rong, Helena. *"Inter-Agent Trust Models: A Comparative Study of Brief, Claim, Proof, Stake, Reputation and Constraint in Agentic Web Protocol Design — A2A, AP2, ERC-8004, and Beyond."* arXiv:2511.03434, 2025.

**Real, verified status: arXiv preprint, submitted to the AAAI 2026 Workshop on Trust and Control in Agentic AI (TrustAgent).** A workshop submission is real and does typically involve some review, but it is a lighter-weight process than a full conference or journal, and — as of this writing — no confirmation was found that it has actually been accepted/presented yet, only that it was submitted.

**Do not cite this as a "Q1 journal citation."** Cite as: *Hu & Rong, arXiv preprint 2511.03434 (submitted to AAAI 2026 TrustAgent workshop; workshop acceptance not independently confirmed).*

### 4. Kroehl, Lars Kersten. *"Trust Without Trusting: A Recomputable Trust Protocol for Autonomous Agents"* (MolTrust). arXiv:2605.06738, 2026.

**Real, verified status: arXiv preprint only** (v1 and a real v2 dated June 14, 2026). No peer-reviewed journal or conference publication found. The paper describes a real, production-deployed reference implementation (operational since March 2026, on-chain anchoring on Base) — real, substantive engineering work — but that is a separate claim from peer-reviewed publication status, which is what's being verified here.

### 5. Anand, Atul; Chattaraj, Sourav. *"Diagnosing Tool-Selection Reasoning in LLM Agents with Canary Tools."* arXiv:2608.04719, 2026.

**Real, verified status: arXiv preprint only**, and a very recent one (submitted August 5, 2026). No peer-reviewed publication found — expected, given how recently it was posted.
